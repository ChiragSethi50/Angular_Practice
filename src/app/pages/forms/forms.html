Using Forms

Forms are used to collect user data

There are two main types of forms in angular and one experimental form.

  • Reactive forms: Provide better usage and validations (recommended)
  • Template-Driven forms: Used for only simple forms (not-recommended)
  • Signal forms -> Reactive forms + Signals (Experimental)
